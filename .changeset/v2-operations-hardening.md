`groot resume <operationId> --allow <class>` grants per-run approvals for the steps a resumed operation still runs; resume re-checks the action policy against the journal and refuses a plan copy that is not the plan the operation started. Policy loading fails closed: an unreadable or invalid `groot.json` is `GROOT_E_INVALID_DOCUMENT` instead of silently using the default policy. Plans are integrity-checked (every `produced` expectation must name an earlier step that produces that path; no action may target `.groot/` or `.git/`), toolchain preconditions probe only allowlisted tools, and recovery never removes a `.git` or `.groot` directory. Stored plan copies and backups are private (0600/0700) and conceal secret values generated by earlier operations.
